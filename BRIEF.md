# CardioConnect Future-State Vision Site Brief

## Project Title

The Future of CardioConnect

## Purpose

This site is a future-state storytelling prototype created to help Boston Scientific and Slalom explore how CardioConnect could evolve beyond scheduling and assignment visibility.

The site is not intended to define a committed roadmap, approved build scope, project team, budget, or implementation plan. Its purpose is to make the future vision tangible, support executive alignment, and frame the strategic questions Boston Scientific needs to resolve before advancing the next phase of CardioConnect.

## Strategic Framing

The current CardioConnect MVP establishes a shared foundation for scheduling, assignment visibility, and field coordination. This future-state vision explores how that foundation could expand into a more connected field operating model across the moments before, during, and after a case.

The concept focuses on how CardioConnect could support:

- Field scheduling and coverage decisions
- Case readiness and clinical preparation
- Procedure documentation and completion
- Inventory readiness and replenishment signals
- Territory, account, and leadership insight
- AI-assisted recommendations, summaries, and workflow support

## Business Levers

The site frames the vision around four business levers:

### Cost Avoidance

Connect scheduled cases, documented device use, expiration risk, par levels, and replenishment signals so teams can act before product expires or stock falls below need.

### Capacity Optimization

Give leaders better visibility into rep workload, availability, geography, qualifications, case demand, and coverage pressure so work can be balanced before assuming more headcount is needed.

### Productivity

Reduce manual coordination and duplicate entry across intake, scheduling, prep, documentation, and downstream handoffs.

### Growth Enablement

Turn field activity, procedure volume, coverage patterns, physician context, and account signals into insight that helps teams prioritize customer follow-up.

## Intended Audience

This site was created for strategic conversations with Boston Scientific and Slalom stakeholders, including product, business, field operations, IT, architecture, sales, and experience leaders.

## Evidence Base

The vision is informed by CardioConnect MVP delivery work, user research, usability testing, workflow analysis, field feedback, stakeholder conversations, and product strategy discussions conducted across the CardioConnect effort.

It is intended to be evidence-backed and directionally useful, while still conceptual.

## Narrative Structure

The site walks viewers through:

1. The business levers this future vision could impact
2. A future-state field workflow in motion
3. The MVP foundation and why it matters
4. A maturity model for how CardioConnect could evolve
5. Capability progression from MVP foundation to future horizon
6. Human-centered field impact
7. Downstream business outcomes
8. Implementation considerations across platforms, data, and AI
9. The strategic workstream needed to define what happens next

## What's Next

To make this vision real, Boston Scientific would need to define what CardioConnect becomes beyond scheduling.

That requires a focused future-state workstream to resolve strategic questions, validate the highest-value workflows, assess platform and data implications, and produce the inputs needed for roadmap planning and future development decisions.

Key questions include:

- Which franchises should be prioritized?
- How far should CardioConnect extend beyond scheduling?
- Which capabilities should remain in ServiceMax Go, live in Salesforce, or follow another platform approach?
- How should current backlog priorities align with the long-term vision?
- What workflows need to be standardized across divisions?
- Where is division-specific flexibility required?
- What data, integration, security, and offline requirements must be resolved?
- Where can AI safely assist, recommend, summarize, or prefill?
