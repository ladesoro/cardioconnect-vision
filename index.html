Where the vision matters

The best technology fades into the rhythm of the work.

The future of CardioConnect should not feel like another thing for reps and leaders to manage. It should be a connected layer that fits naturally into field work — helping teams get to the right procedure, with the right context, at the right time, so they can support physicians and reach more patients.

Implementation reality

Future exploration should honor the stack BSC already has.

This vision is not a blanket replacement story. It is a way to evaluate which layers should remain packaged, which should integrate more deeply, and where custom or AI-enabled experiences could create differentiated value.

1 Start with what works

Retain and leverage

Preserve the enterprise foundations, investments, and trusted sources that should continue to anchor the experience.

  • Extend the CardioConnect scheduling and assignment foundation
  • Leverage Salesforce data, identity, access, and security controls
  • Reuse proven procedure and enterprise data models
  • Build on existing ETL, API, and enterprise integration capabilities
  • Preserve product, physician, facility, and inventory systems of record
2 Define the right fit

Evaluate and integrate

Assess the systems, workflow boundaries, and experience decisions required for the next connected lifecycle.

  • Define cross-division procedure workflows and shared requirements
  • Integrate CRM IRIS REX capabilities with the shared procedure data model
  • Review AFS procedure workflows and field documentation needs
  • Connect device inventory systems and product context
  • Define patient-record integration and offline data boundaries
  • Assess the BSC Digital Health Platform for tailored application development
3 Enable the horizon

Future enablers

Introduce advanced capabilities as the workflow, architecture, data, and governance become ready.

  • Offline synchronization, recovery, and conflict management
  • Scheduling automation and case-readiness orchestration
  • Barcode, image, and document capture
  • Document extraction, generation, and downstream handoffs
  • Inventory readiness and replenishment signals
  • Governed AI services with observability, auditability, and human review

Next horizon

Preserve the foundation. Extend the lifecycle. Explore the platform path.

A potential path forward

What could we do next?

If this future-state direction feels worth exploring, Slalom could help BSC move from vision to action—validating the highest-value opportunities, pressure-testing the field workflow, and identifying the experience and platform decisions needed for CardioConnect’s next phase.

Engagement objective

Turn the vision into an evidence-backed direction.

The work would help BSC determine which connected field capabilities are worth advancing, how they should work across AFS and CRM, and what experience, data, and platform choices would enable them—without prematurely locking into a solution or implementation path.

Questions to resolve

The decisions behind the vision

The goal is not to validate every idea. It is to identify where a connected experience can create the most meaningful field and business value.

01

Operating model

Which case moments should work consistently across CRM, EP, and Watchman, and where is specialization essential?

02

Experience boundary

Can ServiceMax Go and Salesforce support the desired experience, or does differentiated field work justify a custom mobile layer?

03

System of record

How should work orders, procedures, therapies, devices, teams, and downstream records connect without duplicating truth?

04

Field security

What patient or hospital information is truly required at each moment, and what must never persist offline?

05

Value case

Which outcomes—coverage, documentation quality, inventory waste, rep capacity, or account action—justify investment?

06

AI permission

Where can AI safely recommend, summarize, or prefill—and where must regulated or clinical judgment remain explicitly human?

How we could approach it

Five moves from vision to action

Each move builds evidence for the next, bringing field experience and technical feasibility together as the direction takes shape.

01

Focus

02
