MVP foundation

The MVP is the foundation, not the throwaway.

CardioConnect MVP created the first shared layer for field scheduling visibility — bringing work intake, assignment, mobile access, and team coordination into a more consistent operating model.

01
Shared work visibility

Procedures, non-procedure activity, and unavailable time can be represented as field work that teams can see and coordinate around.

02
Assignment operating model

Coverage decisions, reassignment, team calendar views, and field mobile access establish the behaviors needed for future intelligence.

03
Platform learning

The MVP clarifies where packaged capabilities fit — and where the next horizon may require deeper integration, automation, or custom experiences.

Current foundation

Human field moment

Technology matters most when it disappears into the work.

The future of CardioConnect is not another destination for field teams to manage. It is a connected operating layer that supports the moments before, during, and after each case.

What connected work makes possible

Downstream value appears when the case lifecycle is connected.

Once scheduling, readiness, and documentation share context, field activity can begin informing inventory, account, and leadership decisions.

Continue

Inventory readiness

Confirmed device use can reduce expiration risk, update account stock position, and trigger replenishment before the next case is at risk.

  • Expiration opportunity surfaced before use
  • Below-par condition created after documentation
  • Replenishment request prepared from case context
Act

Account signals

Procedure activity, urgent add-ons, coverage needs, and inventory follow-through can become territory signals instead of disconnected reports.

  • Methodist surfaces from urgent CRM activity
  • Physician outreach can be prioritized
  • Field events become account context
Lead

Leadership visibility

Managers and leaders can see demand, workload, readiness risk, and follow-through across teams without relying solely on retrospective reporting.

  • Coverage gaps and capacity concerns visible earlier
  • Team workload balancing becomes actionable
  • Operational health can be monitored by region

Implementation reality

This vision is not a blanket replacement story. It is a way to evaluate which layers should remain packaged, which should integrate more deeply, and where custom or AI-enabled experiences could create differentiated value.
